# Product Specification — RITKARA VEYRA

## Product Overview
**RITKARA VEYRA** is a premium strategy and technology consultancy serving local businesses and growing startups across India and beyond. The firm acts as an integrated digital partner, coordinating digital presence, customer acquisition, lead and sales workflows, business automation, analytics, and ongoing technical management.

- **Working Brand**: RITKARA VEYRA
- **Tagline**: MAKE COMPLEXITY WORK.
- **Operating Entity / Legal Notice**: Primeora Solutions (Udyam Registered MSME: `UDYAM-KR-03-0732181`). Ritkara Veyra is the working brand under which client engagements and new service propositions are introduced.

---

## Target Audience & Problem Statement
- **Audience**: Small and mid-size enterprises, local businesses, professional clinics, retail businesses, and owner-led startups that have outgrown fragmented freelance solutions and need an integrated, dependable digital partner.
- **Problem**: Business owners waste time and budget juggling separate web developers, SEO freelancers, ad agencies, and automation tools that don't connect. Leads get lost between channels, operations remain manual, and reporting is non-existent.
- **Value Proposition**: A cohesive, engineering-led "Digital A-Z" system that connects visibility to lead capture, operations to automation, and performance data to practical decisions.

4. **Operations & Automation**
   - Cross-platform business workflow automation
   - Practical AI-assisted task workflows

   - Executive Business Intelligence (BI) dashboards
   - Multi-touch lead and sales tracking
   - Monthly performance reporting
   - Actionable interpretation of key business metrics
6. **Ongoing Management**
   - Proactive website maintenance & uptime monitoring
   - Content updates, design refreshes, and seasonal adjustments
   - Technical security updates, vulnerability patching, and backups
   - Client team training, documentation, and handover
   - Continuous conversion and performance optimization

## Service Packages
- **Launch**: Essential digital foundation for emerging businesses (website, branding basics, GBP, initial CRM/WhatsApp setup, baseline SEO).
- **Grow**: Targeted at businesses ready to scale inbound enquiries (everything in Launch + local SEO/AEO, social content system, follow-up automation, monthly reporting).
- **Complete Partner**: Full digital ownership and dedicated continuous improvement (everything in Grow + ongoing maintenance, content/ads support, continuous optimization, priority response).

*Guardrail: All package scopes, turnaround times, third-party software subscriptions, and deliverables are confirmed per client agreement. No fabricated prices or unsubstantiated guarantees.*

## Core System Requirements
- **CMS & Directory**: Secure, database-backed team management system with role-based permissions (`super_admin`, `content_editor`), draft/publish workflows, and profile editing.
- **Enquiry Pipeline**: High-accessibility enquiry form with CSRF/honeypot bot rejection, rate limiting, data sanitization, and administrative review queue.
- **SEO/AEO**: Semantic HTML5, JSON-LD structured data, dynamic XML sitemap, canonical links, robots controls, and AEO-targeted FAQs.
- **Privacy & Security**: Granular cookie consent banner (Accept / Reject / Preferences), GDPR/DPDP-aligned Privacy Policy, strict Content Security Policy, zero committed secrets.
